Research

When do simple decision rules perform well, when do they break down, and why?

  • Stochastic systems & queueing. Understanding and improving the performance of service systems under uncertainty.
  • Sequential learning & decisions. Learning from data to make better decisions over time.
  • Stochastic model reduction. Developing tractable models that capture the essential features of complex stochastic systems.
