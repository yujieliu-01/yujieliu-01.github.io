Research

When do simple decision rules perform well, when do they break down, and why?

  • Stochastic systems & queueing. Finite-time performance, control policies, and the role of information in congested service systems.
  • Sequential learning & decisions. How adaptive algorithms allocate samples, and how action geometry shapes learning and exploration.
  • Stochastic model reduction. Finding simpler Markovian representations that preserve the distributional structure of complex systems.
