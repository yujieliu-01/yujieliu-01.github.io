About Me

I am a postdoctoral research fellow in the Department of Industrial Systems Engineering and Management at the National University of Singapore, supervised by Yunbei Xu. I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research from the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science at the Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Dacheng Yao and Hanqin Zhang.

My research interests lie in stochastic modeling, queueing systems, and online learning, with a focus on understanding and improving decision-making under uncertainty in operational systems.
