DECISIONS UNDER UNCERTAINTY

About me

I study how information, learning, and system dynamics shape decision-making under uncertainty.

I am a postdoctoral research fellow in the Department of Industrial Systems Engineering and Management at the National University of Singapore, working with Yunbei Xu and Vincent Y. F. Tan. Previously, I worked with Shuangchi He at NUS.

I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research from the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Dacheng Yao and Hanqin Zhang, and my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Jilin University.
