About Me

I am a postdoctoral research fellow in the Department of Industrial Systems Engineering and Management at the National University of Singapore, supervised by Yunbei Xu. I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research from the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science at the Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Dacheng Yao and Hanqin Zhang.

Research Interests

My research focuses on decision-making under uncertainty in operational systems, with the goal of understanding and improving system performance. I am especially drawn to simple, canonical models that capture the essential features of an operational problem. My research is rooted in applied probability, particularly stochastic modeling and queueing theory, and integrates perspectives from modern sequential learning.

  • Stochastic Modeling
  • Sequential Learning
